We are a duo of acoustic guitar and hurdy-gurdy based in Mexico and the UK. We play long form, dark, mantra-like improvisations on acoustic guitar and hurdy-gurdy. Drawing on folk traditions, drones and repetition, our music sometimes feels like an old folk ritual, sometimes like something coming apart at the seams.
